Research Overview

I am a PhD candidate at Beijing Institute of Technology. I study how AI can support industrial software and manufacturing, with a focus on code generation, controllable multimodal generation, and agent reliability. My research focuses on the controllability and reliability of generative AI in industrial settings. I study how to train models to produce outputs that align with user intent and satisfy domain-specific constraints. I also use agent harnesses to improve reliability on long-horizon and complex tasks.

Before my PhD, I earned a master’s degree at the University of Delaware and worked as a full-stack engineer at IC Realtime in the US and at the 50th Research Institute of China Electronics Technology Group Corporation. My work included multi-stream video previews, on-device video processing, integration of AI recognition and inference services, backend data services, and frontend development.
